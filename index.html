The promise

Perform for Life is a team of strength and conditioning specialists. Your coach starts with your history, your schedule, and what you want to be able to do outside the studio.

Nothing here comes off a shelf. The work is built for you, and it changes as you do.

Providing expertise & results

How we decide what you do

We assess before we load.Then we keep loading.

  1. First

    Nothing gets loaded until we have watched you move it. Your first session is mostly us looking.

    It is the least satisfying hour you will spend here.

  2. Then

    A joint you cannot take through full range does not get weight on it yet. The range comes first.

    Which sometimes means a month of work you did not come here for.

  3. Only then

    Load goes on, and it keeps going on. The plan is written to get heavier, not to stay comfortable.

    We write it down so you can see the difference in March.

  4. Alongside

    The chiropractor's table is in the building, not in a referral. Treatment and training stay on one plan.

    You tell the story once.

  5. Throughout

    Mental strength gets coached the way a lift does: planned, practised, progressed.

    One of our coaches is certified for it. It is not a pep talk.

Then we measure again. Gait on the slow-motion camera, oxygen on the mask, the same numbers we took in week one. If they have not moved, the plan was wrong and we change it.
